About

The developer behind the code.

I'm Mariam — a frontend developer and business website designer based in Nigeria, working with businesses and individuals who want a website that actually earns trust the moment someone lands on it.

My focus is simple: build websites for real businesses — restaurants, salons, retail, service providers — that need to look credible, load fast, and work perfectly on the phone screens most of their customers are actually using. I care as much about how a site performs on a cracked mid-range Android in a moving car as I do about how it looks on a 27-inch monitor.

I build with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I use AI tools deliberately in my workflow — the same way any modern developer uses a compiler, a linter or a component library. I don't ship code I don't understand. Every project I take on gets read line by line, rebuilt where it needs to be, and customised end-to-end to the client's brand, content and goals before it ever goes live.

What I'm building toward is a small studio practice: fewer, better projects, real relationships with the businesses I build for, and a portfolio that's honest about what's a live client site and what's a concept built to show what's possible.

Process

How a project comes together.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Understanding your business, your customers and what the site actually needs to accomplish before any design begins.

  2. 02

    Wireframe

    Mapping out structure and content — what goes where, and why — so the design has a clear foundation.

  3. 03

    Design

    Building the visual identity: colour, type, imagery and layout, tailored to your brand rather than a template.

  4. 04

    Development

    Turning the design into clean, semantic, responsive code that performs well on every device.

  5. 05

    Testing

    Checking every page across browsers, screen sizes and real devices before anything goes live.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Deploying your site and handing over a clear, working product — with support available afterward.
